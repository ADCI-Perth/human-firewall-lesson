title: "Contributor Code of Conduct"
---

This lesson is a companion to an ADCI microcredential. It is not an official Carpentries lesson, and The Carpentries does not receive reports about it.

As contributors and maintainers of this project, we pledge to treat everyone with respect, whatever their background or experience. This code is adapted from [The Carpentries Code of Conduct][coc], which is licensed [CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming, patient and kind.
- Give and accept feedback on the lesson politely and with a focus on the work.
- Respect different views, experience and ways of learning.
- Take care with real people: use made-up examples, and do not share anyone's personal information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in any form.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Anything else that a reasonable person would see as abusive or as making others unwelcome.

## Reporting

To report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour, contact the lesson maintainers at [ADCI contact to confirm]. Reports are handled in confidence where possible. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may ask people to stop.

[coc]: https://docs.carpentries.org/topic_folders/policies/code-of-conduct.html
