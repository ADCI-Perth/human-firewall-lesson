---
title: Setup
---

There is nothing to install for this lesson.

## What you need

- A web browser, to read the episodes.
- A notebook or a blank document, to write down your answers to the activities.
- About 45 to 60 minutes for each episode. The episodes are short and stand on their own, so you can do one at a time.

## If you are teaching it

You can run any episode in person or online with the same materials. Instructors will find a plan and notes for each episode on the instructor pages. A shared whiteboard or a flip chart is useful for the discussion prompts, but it is not required.

## Before you start

Think of one small organisation you know well, such as a workplace, a club or a community group. Several activities ask you to compare the example organisation with your own.
