## Contributing

This lesson is a companion to an ADCI microcredential. It is not an official Carpentries lesson. Suggestions, fixes and bug reports are welcome.

### Contributor agreement

By contributing, you agree that we may redistribute your work under [our licence](LICENSE.md). Everyone taking part agrees to follow the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

### How to contribute

1. **Report a problem or suggest a change.** If you have a GitHub account, open an issue in this repository. Otherwise, contact the lesson maintainers at [ADCI contact to confirm].
2. **Change the lesson.** If you are comfortable with Git, make a branch, commit your change and open a pull request. Describe what you changed and why.

Before you open a pull request, build the lesson and run the checks described in the [README](README.md): `bash scripts/build.sh`, then the three `scripts/check-*.sh` scripts. The lesson is built with [The Carpentries Workbench][template-doc].

### What to contribute

Fixes to wording, facts, links and examples are the most useful. If you propose new material, say how long it would take to teach and what you would take out to make room, because each episode is designed for 45 to 60 minutes.

Keep to the lesson's style: Australian English, plain language, made-up examples rather than real people, and Harvard references for any source. Do not add paywalled text.

### Reviews

Comments on issues and reviews of pull requests are welcome. Reviews from newcomers are especially valuable, because they show where the material is hard to follow.

The maintainers decide what is merged.
