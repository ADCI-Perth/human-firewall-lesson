title: Learner Profiles
---

These two people show who this lesson is written for. They are fictional.

## Mia, a small-business employee

Mia works in the front office of a family-run physiotherapy clinic with eight staff. She books appointments, takes payments and keeps client records. Nobody in the clinic has a security role. Mia has noticed more strange emails lately and is not sure what is safe to open. She wants practical habits that fit into a busy day, plain explanations of her duties under privacy law, and the confidence to say "that does not look right" to her manager.

**What she needs from the lesson:** short episodes, no jargon, and activities based on realistic workplace situations.

## Daniel, an ADCI instructor

Daniel teaches on the microcredential at ADCI. He is not a security specialist. He runs optional live sessions for registered students and wants material he can teach in a single session. He cares about accurate references and activities that work without special software.

**What he needs from the lesson:** instructor notes, timings he can trust and answers he can check.
